Google Form Integration Guide

  1. Create a new Google Form in your Google account.
  2. Add required fields: Parent Name, Student Name, Grade, Email, Phone, Goals/Concerns, Current Resources, Why DDA Tutoring?
  3. Click Send, then choose:
    • Link option: paste form URL into the “Open Sign-up Form” button above.
    • Embed option: copy iframe HTML and paste it into this section if preferred.
  4. In Google Forms, open Responses and connect to Google Sheets using the green Sheets icon.
  5. New submissions will sync automatically to your connected spreadsheet.
